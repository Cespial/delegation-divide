What ADI adds lies elsewhere, and four results locate it. First, the level scale: exposure indices say whether AI touches a task, while ADI separates partial from full delegation, and full delegation turns out to be rare (% of world task time) and concentrated in a few clerical groups (Chapter 4). Second, the barriers, which say why a task stops short of L3 and which are not recorded by any exposure index. Third, the reach: a task-level index carried to countries in ISCO-08, where exposure indices are mostly confined to the United States. Two cautions apply.
